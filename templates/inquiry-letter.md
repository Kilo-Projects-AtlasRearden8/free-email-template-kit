# Inquiry Letter

Fill in the bracketed fields, then copy the text below into your email.
Delete the brackets and any helper text before sending.

**Subject:** Question about [Topic / Product / Position / Opportunity]

Dear [Recipient Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I'm writing to ask about [what you want to know].
[One sentence about who you are or why you're asking, e.g. I'm a reader of your
newsletter / I saw your listing on the website / I'm exploring options for my
small business.]

Could you please help with the following?

- [Question 1]
- [Question 2]
- [Question 3]

If there's a better person to contact, or a page on your site that covers this,
I'd appreciate a pointer.

Thank you for your time,

[Your Name]
[Your Email address]
[Your phone number, optional]
