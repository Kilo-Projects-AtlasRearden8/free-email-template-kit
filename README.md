# Free Email Template Kit

Fillable email templates for everyday work and life: thank-you notes, inquiry
letters, follow-ups, and polite declines. Plain English, warm tone, ready to
copy and send — no account needed, nothing to install.

Published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana), founded by registered nurse
and AI author Dylan Asdale. Beginner-friendly tools for authors, small business
owners, healthcare workers, and anyone who just wants to write a decent email
without overthinking it. Plain English, step by step, never condescending.

## What's inside

| Template | Markdown source | Printable HTML | Best for |
|----------|-----------------|----------------|----------|
| Thank-you note | `templates/thank-you-note.md` | `html/thank-you-note.html` | Interviews, gifts, favors, someone's time |
| Inquiry letter | `templates/inquiry-letter.md` | `html/inquiry-letter.html` | Questions about a product, role, or opportunity |
| Follow-up | `templates/follow-up.md` | `html/follow-up.html` | Gently bumping a previous message |
| Polite decline | `templates/polite-decline.md` | `html/polite-decline.html` | Saying no without burning bridges |

Every template uses bracketed placeholders like `[Recipient Name]` and
`[Your Name]`. Fill them in, delete the brackets, and send.

## How to use

**Markdown versions** — Open any `.md` file in a text editor (Notepad, TextEdit,
VS Code, etc.), replace each `[bracketed field]` with your own words, delete
the brackets, then copy the text into your email client and send.

**Printable HTML versions** — Open any `.html` file in your browser. Click each
underlined field and type right in the page, or print it (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) and
fill it in by hand. Then print or save to PDF. Each page is styled for clean
printing: no screen chrome, no wasted ink.

No sign-up, no tracking, no personal data collected. Everything runs locally in
your browser or editor.

## Filled-in example

Here's what a finished thank-you note looks like after you fill in the brackets:

```
Subject: Thank you — your time

Hi Maya,

Thank you for speaking with me yesterday. I really appreciated hearing about
the team's approach to patient education.

I'm excited about the next step and look forward to hearing from you.

Thanks again,

Dylan Asdale
dylan@example.com
(555) 123-4567
```

## Printing tips

- Open the HTML file in your browser, then press **Ctrl+P** (Windows/Linux) or
  **Cmd+P** (Mac).
- Choose **Save as PDF** if you'd rather keep a digital copy.
- The underlined fields print as blank lines you can write on by hand.
